## Founder decisions, 2026-09-11

Recorded from the founder's answers of 2026-09-11. They amend the sequence below
and the proposed upstream lock; they do not certify any unexecuted integration.

1. **The local Repo2RLEnv lane runs first.** Its qualification (item 2 below) no
   longer waits on Fabric/Hermes parity, Relay evidence, published evidence facets
   or the Codex path. Those remain v0.2.0 work and their gates are unchanged.
2. **The subject is the user's own local agent.** The first lane evaluates the
   end user's own Hermes installation (later Prime Agent) on tasks generated from
   the user's own repository, with and without a candidate Skill. The Skills under
   study are the family that helps an agent understand a codebase and its
   third-party libraries. A mutable local subject is recorded as such; it is not
   a pinned subject instance and its Results are private local evidence.
3. **Pipelines.** The first qualified recipes are Repo2RLEnv `commit_runtime` and
   `pr_diff`, not `pr_runtime`. Docker may be required.
4. **Codex waits.** The Codex operator and subject follow NeMo Fabric and Relay as
   already planned. No Codex package is created for this lane.
5. **Local only.** The lane produces local files: the Harbor task package, the
   qualification receipt, and local comparison Results. Publication to Techtree
   or any hub is a later, separately approved step.
6. **Upstream lock unfrozen.** The proposed lock in `docs/v0.2/UPSTREAM_CONTRACT_LOCK.json`
   is not binding for this lane. Current upstream releases of Repo2RLEnv,
   Verifiers, Harbor and Hermes may be adopted. The founder will refreeze with a
   new lock packet shortly after; frozen v0.1 release bytes stay untouched.
7. **Model access is the end user's own account.** Generation and evaluation call
   models through the user's local connection: the Hermes portal subscription
   where it works, otherwise the user's ChatGPT subscription. Techtree never
   holds or forwards those credentials; no platform-funded inference is implied.

## Runtime responsibilities

Repo2RLEnv generates Harbor tasks. Prime Agent may author/repair within a bounded
worker and participate through its qualified native Verifiers path; a Fabric
adapter is not a prerequisite for that native path. Verifiers owns evaluation
and canonical reward. Fabric supplies compatibility planning and lifecycle for
admitted harnesses. Relay remains observe-only for the declared evidence profile;
retain native traces, projected-event labels and omissions. Hermes is the existing
Skill-comparison starting point, not evidence that the new combined service works.

A completed qualification must demonstrate clean build/reset, a successful
reference repair, the intended failure of an unrepaired control, actual recognized
test collection, protected verifier material, bounded resources, preserved rejection
reasons and reproduction on a fresh worker. Reject unsupported semantics rather
than substituting a successful exit code for trustworthy evaluation.
