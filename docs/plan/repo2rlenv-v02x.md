# Approved narrow Repo2RLEnv expansion

Status: founder-approved scope amendment, 2026-09-08. Implementation and runtime
qualification remain separate from approval. This document overrides only the
conflicting post-v0.2.0 sequencing and artifact restrictions in `techtree-market.md`.
The execution-foundation contract in `v0.2.md` remains the v0.2.0 priority.

## Release sequence

1. Finish Fabric/Hermes parity, bounded Relay evidence, additional admitted harnesses,
   and published evidence facets. Preserve historical proof bytes and verification.
2. Qualify one local Repo2RLEnv Python/pytest PR-runtime recipe. Pin its source
   history, images, tooling, rights, tasks, scoring and output contract. Treat
   commit-history inputs as a separately qualified recipe.
3. Build organization/capability authorization, immutable environment releases,
   qualifications, public metadata, private storage and repeatable access grants.
4. Add completed-artifact offers/orders and exact payment reconciliation.
   Reconciled stablecoin invoices may precede a qualified x402 integration.
5. Connect bounded quotes and customer authorization to durable isolated Forge
   jobs, cancellation, reconciliation, delivery and access.
6. Add Repo Evaluate against immutable releases and individually admitted subjects.
7. Keep provider training in v0.3 or later, limited initially to one qualified
   recipe/provider/checkpoint family with held-out evaluation.

This moves the narrow Repo Forge lane into v0.2.x; it does not pull a general
Foundry, marketplace, optimization platform or training system into v0.2.0.
Existing similarly named shared-CLI commands do not establish compatibility.
Coordinate `regents techtree` with its owner and keep scientific semantics in Python.

## Admission and authority

- Permit class-specific qualified private artifacts and gated bytes. Complete
  the applicable rights, isolation, retention, egress and access gates before
  accepting private inputs or executing repository code, not merely before launch.
- Remove the private Skill Climb prerequisite for the narrow customer Repo Forge
  service. Its own qualification and delivery contract is sufficient; it need not
  produce positive Skill uplift to be useful or commercially complete.
- Ordinary customer jobs may proceed under explicit customer approvals or bounded
  owner-signed policies. This is not permission to spend platform treasury funds,
  sign for a customer, move money, or run an unbounded repair loop.
- Platform-funded inference, exact upstream release locks, public pilots and
  settlement parameters still require their applicable approvals. The current
  commit/push/site-deploy authorization does not certify new execution lanes.
- Source publication and private-data release require separate owner permission.
  Keep build outcome, qualification, research outcome, payment and access distinct.

## Runtime responsibilities

Repo2RLEnv generates Harbor tasks. Prime Agent may author/repair within a bounded
worker and participate through its qualified native Verifiers path; a Fabric
adapter is not a prerequisite for that native path. Verifiers owns evaluation
and canonical reward. Fabric supplies compatibility planning and lifecycle for
admitted harnesses. Relay remains observe-only for the declared evidence profile;
retain native traces, projected-event labels and omissions. Hermes is the existing
Skill-comparison starting point, not evidence that the new combined service works.

A completed qualification must demonstrate clean build/reset, a successful
reference repair, the intended failure of an unrepaired control, actual recognized
test collection, protected verifier material, bounded resources, preserved rejection
reasons and reproduction on a fresh worker. Reject unsupported semantics rather
than substituting a successful exit code for trustworthy evaluation.

## Completion and public claims

Repo2RLEnv remains Planned until the advertised lane works end to end. No synthetic
fixture, installed dependency, harness descriptor, version selector, signed bundle
or paid delivery establishes real execution, universal compatibility, independent
reproduction or guaranteed improvement. Historical tickets remain reference
material, not an active dispatch system.
