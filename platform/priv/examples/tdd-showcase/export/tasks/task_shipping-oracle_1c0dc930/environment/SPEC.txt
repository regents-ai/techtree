FULFILLMENT SHIPPING QUOTES

The public API is:

    fulfillment.quote.quote(weight_kg, zone, fragile=False)

It returns the shipping charge as an integer number of cents.

Choose the first weight band whose maximum includes the package weight:

- A package weighing up to and including 1 kilogram has a base charge of six hundred cents.
- A package weighing more than 1 kilogram and up to and including 5 kilograms has a base charge of one thousand cents.
- A package weighing more than 5 kilograms and up to and including 20 kilograms has a base charge of eighteen hundred cents.

Apply the multiplier for the destination zone:

- local: one hundred percent
- regional: one hundred twenty-five percent
- remote: one hundred sixty percent

Round the multiplied charge to the nearest cent, with a half cent rounded upward. If the package is fragile, add a surcharge of three hundred fifty cents after applying the zone multiplier.

Worked examples:

- A 1 kilogram local package costs 600 cents.
- A 2 kilogram regional package costs 1250 cents.
- A 5 kilogram remote fragile package costs 1950 cents.
- A 12 kilogram regional fragile package costs 2600 cents.

Callers provide a positive weight no greater than 20 kilograms and one of the three listed zone names.
