# 0035 — v0.1 is a Proof of Concept for the PI-Verifiers / Hermes / Techtree stack

Status: binding (founder ruling, 2026-08-26).

## Ruling

v0.1 is described, publicly and internally, as a **Proof of Concept for the
PI-Verifiers, Hermes and Techtree stack**. Not a benchmark, not a product
release, not a measurement anyone should cite.

The frame is about the *stack*, not only about Techtree. What v0.1
demonstrates is that three independent pieces — Prime Intellect's Verifiers as
the evaluation engine, Hermes as the agent host, and Techtree as the campaign
kernel and evidence layer — can be pinned together tightly enough that a
controlled comparison runs end to end and leaves a receipt that verifies
offline. That is the claim. Nothing about capability, and nothing about how
any of the three performs in general.

## Why this is the right frame

It claims less than what we already say, so it cannot conflict with any
existing copy rule; it settles several of them at once.

The evidence supports it exactly. The task family is synthetic. The Climb
declares itself development-only. The score band is calibrated to one model on
one task set and transfers nowhere. Three executions of an identical arm
scored 24, 23 and 23 of 36, so the one measured improvement of +1 sits inside
the spread and no uplift may be claimed. The proof is participant-attested and
has never been independently reproduced. "Proof of Concept" is the honest name
for that, and every one of those limitations reads as expected rather than
disappointing once the frame is right.

It also names the dependency honestly. We pin an engine, a host and a runtime
image; the release is only as reproducible as those pins. Saying "stack"
rather than "product" tells a reader where the seams are before they find
them.

## What this permits and forbids

- Say: proof of concept, demonstration, development release, the stack works
  end to end, here is a receipt you can verify offline.
- Do not say: benchmark, evaluation suite, measured capability, validated
  uplift, production, or anything that invites comparison between
  participants' results.
- The existing wording boundaries stand unchanged and are not relaxed by this
  ruling: the calibrated band and never an exact score, participant-attested
  and never independently verified, qualified privacy, toy and synthetic
  framing, no price estimate.
- Attribute the parts. Verifiers is Prime Intellect's and Hermes is its own
  project; a proof of concept that implies we built the whole stack is the
  same class of overclaim as any other.

## Where it applies

Public copy on the site, the repositories' READMEs, the plugin's operator
Skill, the release packet and any launch writing. A copy guard extends to it
the way the others do: the claim surfaces that state what this release is must
carry the proof-of-concept frame, and a surface that drops it fails.
